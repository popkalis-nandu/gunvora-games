SETTINGS

HOW TO PLAY

Every night the mansion rearranges its rooms. Find the glowing clues 📜 to unseal the exit door, then escape before sunrise.

Zombies can be killed with your blessed revolver. Ghosts are immune to bullets — avoid them, or banish them with the Spirit Lantern. Ghosts sense you from further away when your flashlight is on.

Some bookshelves hide secret passages to treasure rooms. Look for shelves that shimmer and press USE. Secrets you find shape your ending.

Every 10th night, the Immortal Master waits in the Grand Hall. He cannot die while his soul urns burn — shoot them first.
