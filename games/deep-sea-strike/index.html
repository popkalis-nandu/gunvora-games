HOW TO PLAY

Fight through 10 oceans × 25 levels = 250 unique missions: destroy fleets, beat the clock, escort convoys, defend bases, hunt stealth subs, chase couriers, run minefields, sink battleships and storm fortresses. A boss guards every 10th level and each ocean's final level. Level 100 is the OCEAN MASSACRE; level 250 is the supreme final boss.
