How activation works

  1. Click Subscribe — the secure payment page opens.
  2. Pay by UPI, card, net-banking or wallet.
  3. You receive an unlock code by email (usually within minutes; at most 24 hours).
  4. Enter it on the Redeem page. Premium activates instantly on that browser/device.

Changed device or cleared your browser data? Email us your payment ID and we'll send a fresh code.

FAQ

Will I be charged automatically every month?

No. Plans are one-time payments for a fixed period (30 or 365 days). When it ends you simply go back to the Free plan — nothing is deducted automatically.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Because plans don't auto-renew there is nothing to cancel. Refunds are available in the cases listed in our Refund & Cancellation Policy (e.g. duplicate payment or if we can't deliver your code).

Are Kids games affected?

Kids games are always free, ad-free and have no purchases — for every player.

Do in-game items give an advantage?

No. Store items are cosmetic only (skins, paints, outfits). Nobody can pay to win.

Is my under-18 child allowed to buy?

Purchases must be made by an adult, or by a minor only with the verifiable consent of a parent/guardian. See our Privacy Policy.
