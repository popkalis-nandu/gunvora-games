2. Why we use it

  • To provide games, deliver unlock codes and activate purchases (performance of your request / consent).
  • To answer support, refund and grievance requests.
  • To keep accounting and tax records as required by Indian law.
  • To show advertising and measure site usage — only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.
  • To prevent fraud, secure the Site and comply with legal obligations (legitimate uses under Section 7 of the DPDP Act).

We process personal data only for the purpose for which it was given, and we will ask for fresh consent before using it for anything new. Each consent request is presented in clear, plain language.

3. Children's data & parental consent

Some of our games are designed for young players. Under the DPDP Act, a "child" is anyone under 18.

  • Our Kids games (e.g. Dino Dash, Memory Jungle) show no advertisements, contain no purchases and collect no personal data.
  • We do not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children.
  • Purchases and contact forms are intended for adults. If you are under 18, you must have the verifiable consent of your parent or lawful guardian before making a payment or sharing personal data with us. We may ask for confirmation of such consent.
  • If a parent or guardian believes their child has given us personal data without consent, please contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete it promptly and, where appropriate, refund an unauthorised purchase in line with our Refund Policy.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with Data Processors who help us run the Site, under contracts requiring them to protect it:

  • Payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd., Stripe) — to process payments; their own privacy policies apply on their pages.
  • Hosting / CDN providers (e.g. GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare) — to serve the Site.
  • Email providers — to send unlock codes and support replies.
  • Advertising partners (e.g. Google AdSense) — only if you accept advertising cookies, and never on Kids games.
  • Government authorities or courts when required by law.

5. Retention

Support messages are kept for up to 24 months. Transaction records are retained for as long as required by applicable tax, GST and accounting laws (typically up to 8 years). Other data is deleted once its purpose is served or you withdraw consent, unless law requires otherwise. On-device data stays in your browser until you clear it (you can use "Erase my local data" on the Redeem page).

6. Transfers outside India

Some processors may store data on servers outside India. Such transfers are made in accordance with Section 16 of the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Government of India.

7. Security

We take reasonable security safeguards (HTTPS, limited access, reputable PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways) to prevent personal data breaches. If a breach occurs, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required by law.

8. Your rights

As a Data Principal you have the right to: (a) obtain a summary of your personal data and processing (Sec. 11); (b) correction, completion, updating and erasure (Sec. 12); (c) grievance redressal (Sec. 13); (d) nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity (Sec. 14); and (e) withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (Sec. 6). To exercise any right, email support@example.com or our Grievance Officer. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
