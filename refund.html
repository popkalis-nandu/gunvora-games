Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy applies to Premium plans and in-game items purchased on Gaming World of Jarvis from Novature HRMS. All products are digital goods delivered electronically.

1. Cancellation

  • Premium plans are one-time payments for a fixed period (30 or 365 days) and do not auto-renew, so no cancellation is needed — your plan simply ends.
  • If any plan is offered as an auto-renewing subscription in future, you can cancel at any time before the next renewal date via the link in your payment confirmation or by emailing info@novature.in; you keep benefits until the end of the paid period.
  • You may cancel an order before your unlock code is redeemed by emailing us within 7 days of payment; you will receive a full refund.

2. When you are eligible for a refund

  • Duplicate or double payment for the same order.
  • Payment was deducted but we failed to deliver your unlock code within 48 hours and cannot deliver it after you contact us.
  • A technical problem on our side prevents you from using what you bought and we cannot fix it within 7 days.
  • Unredeemed codes, if requested within 7 days of payment.
  • Purchases made by a minor without parental consent, where a parent/guardian requests it within 7 days and the item has not been substantially used.

3. When refunds are not available

  • Change of mind after the unlock code has been redeemed (digital content is delivered instantly).
  • Partially used plan periods (except as required by law).
  • Entitlements revoked for fraud, code sharing or breach of our Terms.
  • Loss of items due to clearing browser data — instead, we will re-issue your code free of charge.

4. How to request a refund

Email info@novature.in with subject "Refund request", including your payment ID, the email/phone used at checkout, date and reason. We acknowledge within 48 hours.

5. Processing time

Approved refunds are initiated within 3 business days and credited to the original payment method within 5–7 business days (bank/UPI timelines may vary). Once refunded, the related code and entitlements are deactivated.

6. Failed transactions

If money was debited but the payment failed, it is normally auto-reversed by your bank or the payment gateway within 5–7 business days. Contact us if it isn't.
