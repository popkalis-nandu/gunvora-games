How to file a complaint

  1. Email the Grievance Officer with the subject "Grievance".
  2. Include your name, contact details, a description of the issue, relevant URLs or screenshots, and payment ID (if related to a purchase).
  3. For privacy requests (access, correction, erasure, consent withdrawal), mention "DPDP request".

Timelines

  • Acknowledgement: within 24 hours.
  • Resolution: within 15 days, and in any case within the time required by applicable law.
  • Requests to remove unlawful content are acted upon within the timelines prescribed by the IT Rules, 2021.

Escalation

If you're not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India (for personal-data matters) or the appropriate Consumer Commission / National Consumer Helpline (1915 / consumerhelpline.gov.in) for consumer matters.
