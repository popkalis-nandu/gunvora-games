Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how GUNVORA GAMES uses cookies and similar technologies such as browser local storage. When you first visit, a banner lets you choose Accept all or Essential only. You can change your choice any time with (also in the footer).

1. Essential storage (always on)

These are stored only in your browser and are not used to track you:

NamePurposeDuration
gv_consentRemembers your cookie choiceUntil cleared
gv_ent, gv_usedPremium status, owned & equipped items, redeemed codesUntil cleared
gv_best_*, gv_playsYour high scores and play countsUntil cleared
gv_mutedSound on/off preferenceUntil cleared

2. Advertising & analytics cookies (only with consent)

If you click "Accept all", we may load advertising partners such as Google AdSense, which set their own cookies to show and measure ads. See how Google uses cookies. These are never loaded on Kids games or for Premium members.

3. Payment partner cookies

When you open a payment link, the payment gateway (e.g. Razorpay / Stripe) may set cookies on its own website for security and fraud prevention, under its own policy.

4. Managing cookies

Use "Cookie settings" to change consent, "Erase my local data" on the Redeem page to clear GUNVORA data, or your browser settings to block/delete cookies. Blocking essential storage may stop premium status and scores from being saved.
